Where the pit is

Used once, when you press the button, to turn the address into a coordinate pair. Rainfall is then asked for by coordinates and the address never leaves this server again.

Rounded to two decimal places, about a kilometer. Every rainfall model in use is coarser than that, so the rounding costs nothing that could be measured and keeps the request off the building.
