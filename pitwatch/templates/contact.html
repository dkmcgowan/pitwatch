If you are getting text messages

You do not have to reach anybody to stop them. Reply STOP to any message and they end immediately. That is handled by the mobile carriers rather than by this system, so it works whatever else is going on. Replying HELP returns assistance details.

To stop email alerts, reply to any one of them. To be removed entirely, {% if site.contact_email %}ask at the address above{% else %}ask the person who added you{% endif %} and it will be done.
