{# Amps and the contacts, and nothing derived from voltage. The Shelly's own supply is a 120 V outlet, not a tap off the phase the clamps are around, so its voltage reading belongs to a different circuit. Current does not care: a CT measures the field around a conductor and is independent of what the meter is using as a voltage reference. Watts and power factor very much do care, because real power needs voltage and current from the same phase with the right angle between them, and there is no reason to think that angle is right here. So they are not shown. Run history and the average that ignores inrush come from pump_run, which the run detector does not fill in yet. The run contact and the overload used to sit here too, and the disagreement between the contact and the clamp was the useful part: a closed contactor drawing nothing is a motor that is not turning. Putting them back needs a way to say which input carries pump 1's run contact, and inputs are free text labels now. See NOTES.md. #}
