What the display says

The two words in the middle of the dashboard work the way the controller does. Whichever pump ran last sits out the next call, so if pump 1 ran last then pump 2 is LEAD. That still reads correctly while pump 1 is running, because the question the word answers is which pump goes next.

An overload outranks that. A pump whose overload has tripped reads FAIL, and the other one is lead whether or not it was next in the rotation, because it is the only one left.

Until one of the run contacts has been seen to close, both read --. Which pump is lead is the controller's business and it does not tell us, so this waits rather than guessing.
