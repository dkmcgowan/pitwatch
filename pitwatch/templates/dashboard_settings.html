What the display says

The two words in the middle of the dashboard read the way the controller on the wall does. A running pump is LEAD and holds that until its contact drops out, at which point it becomes LAG and the other one is lead for the next call.

Both running is ON and ON. That is the high water case, where the pit has come up past the lag float and the controller has called both. Neither is leading anything at that point.

An overload outranks all of it. A tripped pump reads FAIL and the other is lead whether or not it was next in the rotation, because it is the only one left.

Until a run contact has been seen to close, both read --. Which pump is lead is the controller's business and it does not tell us, so this waits rather than guessing.
