About the alerts this system sends

{{ site.name }} uses PitWatch to watch its sewage ejector pumps. It sends email and text message alerts to a small number of people responsible for the building, and only when the equipment needs attention.

  • Operational alerts only. No marketing, ever.
  • Recipients are added by the building administrator, with their agreement. There is no public sign up and no purchased list.
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts to that number. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Contact details are never sold, rented or shared.
