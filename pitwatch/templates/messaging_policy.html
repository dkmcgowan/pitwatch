Messaging and email policy

Last updated {{ policy_updated }}

PitWatch monitors {{ site.public_pumps_at }}. This page explains what messages it sends, who receives them, how someone consents to receive them, and how to stop.

What these messages are

Every message is an operational alert about the pump equipment. There is no marketing, no promotion, and nothing is ever sold, rented or shared. A message is sent only when the equipment needs attention, for example:

  • A pump has drawn more current than it should for long enough to matter.
  • A motor overload has tripped.
  • The high water float in the pit is wet.
  • Both pumps are running at once.
  • A pump has run far longer than a normal cycle.
  • The monitoring equipment has stopped reporting.

A matching message is sent when the condition clears, so that nobody is left believing a problem is still open when it is not.

How someone starts receiving them

Recipients are added by the building administrator, who enters their name, email address and mobile number, and records whether that person has agreed to receive email, text messages, or both. Nobody is added without asking them first, and consent is not a condition of anything else.

Alerts are sent only to people recorded this way. There is no public sign up form, no purchased list, and no way for a phone number to arrive here except by somebody typing it in on behalf of a person who agreed to it.

How to stop receiving them

Text messages: reply STOP to any message to stop all further text messages to that number. Replying HELP returns contact details for assistance. These replies are handled by the mobile carriers and take effect regardless of what is configured here.

Email: reply to any alert, or ask the building administrator, and the address will be removed.

You can also ask the administrator to turn off either channel, or to remove you entirely, at any time and for any reason.

Frequency

Messages are event driven, not scheduled. In normal operation the equipment is quiet and no messages are sent at all, sometimes for months. During a fault, several may arrive in a short period. Repeats of the same condition are suppressed, so a fault that comes and goes does not send a message every time it flickers.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Names, email addresses and phone numbers are held only to send these alerts. They are stored on the building's own system, are not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and are not used for any other purpose. Phone numbers are passed to the messaging provider solely in order to deliver a message to them. Removal is on request. See the privacy notice.

Getting in touch

You never have to reach anybody to stop these messages. Replying STOP to any text message ends them immediately, and is handled by the mobile carriers rather than by this system, so it works whatever else is going on. Replying HELP returns assistance details. To stop email, reply to any alert.
