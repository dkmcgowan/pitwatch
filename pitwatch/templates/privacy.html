Privacy notice

Last updated {{ policy_updated }}

PitWatch monitors {{ site.pumps_at }}. This notice covers the personal information it holds, which is only what it needs in order to tell somebody that a pump needs attention.

What is held

  • Name, so an alert can be addressed to a person.
  • Email address, if that person wants email alerts.
  • Mobile number, if that person wants text alerts.
  • Whether they want each of those, and a record of when messages were sent to them, so a missed alert can be investigated.
  • For anyone who signs in, a password, stored only as an Argon2 hash which cannot be turned back into the password.

Everything else PitWatch records is about equipment: electrical current, float switch positions, when pumps ran. None of it is about people.

Where it is held

On the building's own system, in its own database. It is not hosted by a third party, and it is not sold, rented or shared. Phone numbers and email addresses are passed to the messaging and email providers only to deliver a message, and for no other purpose.

How long it is kept

Contact details are kept until somebody asks to be removed, or until the administrator removes them. Equipment readings are kept on a rolling schedule and are not personal information.

Your choices

You can ask at any time to see what is held about you, to correct it, to stop either kind of message, or to be removed entirely. Replying STOP to a text message stops text messages immediately, without needing to ask anybody.

See also the messaging policy.
