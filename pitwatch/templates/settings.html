SMS

Amazon SNS is the real one. Texting a US number needs a registered 10DLC or toll-free number on the account, which takes days to set up and is a rule about US messaging rather than anything to do with AWS. Every paid provider has the same requirement. The carrier gateway emails an address like 5551234567@vtext.com and costs nothing, but it is unauthenticated, delivered whenever the carrier feels like it, and being withdrawn. Worth having as a second path to a phone. Not worth being the only one.
