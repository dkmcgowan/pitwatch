SMS Program

PitWatch sends transactional and informational SMS alerts related to building equipment, including pump faults, high-water conditions, maintenance events, equipment status changes, and recovery notifications.

Message frequency varies based on equipment events. Msg & data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. For additional assistance, contact {% if site.contact_email %}{{ site.contact_email }}{% else %}the building administrator who added you to these alerts{% endif %}.

Participation is voluntary. Recipients are enrolled only after providing consent. PitWatch does not send marketing or promotional SMS messages.

See our Privacy Policy at {{ privacy_url }}.

What the alerts are

PitWatch monitors {{ site.public_pumps_at }}. Every message is an operational alert about that equipment, sent because it needs attention:

  • Pump faults. A motor overload has tripped, or a pump has drawn more current than it should for long enough to matter.
  • High-water conditions. The high water float in the pit is wet, meaning water is arriving faster than the pumps are clearing it.
  • Equipment status changes. Both pumps running at once, a pump running far longer than a normal cycle, or a pump that has not run when it should have.
  • Maintenance events. The monitoring hardware has stopped reporting, or a pump's typical load has drifted far enough from its own history to be worth looking at.
  • Recovery notifications. A matching message when a condition clears, so that nobody is left believing a problem is still open when it is not.

Consent and enrollment

Recipients give consent before they are enrolled, and can withdraw it at any time. Somebody is added by the building administrator, who enters their name and mobile number and records that the person has agreed to receive text messages. Nobody is added without being asked first, and consent to text messages is not a condition of anything else.

There is no public sign up form, no purchased list, and no way for a mobile number to reach this system except by somebody entering it on behalf of a person who agreed to it. Withdrawing consent takes effect on request and needs no reason.

How to stop

STOP ends all further text messages to that number. HELP returns contact details for assistance. Both replies are handled by the mobile carriers and take effect regardless of what is configured here, so neither depends on this system being reachable or working. You can also ask the building administrator to remove you entirely, at any time and for any reason. To stop email alerts, reply to any of them.

Message frequency

Messages are event driven, not scheduled. In normal operation the equipment is quiet and no messages are sent at all, sometimes for months. During a fault, several may arrive in a short period. Repeats of the same condition are suppressed, so a fault that comes and goes does not send a message every time it flickers.

Cost

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Names, email addresses and mobile numbers are held only to send these alerts. They are stored on the building's own system and are not used for any other purpose. Mobile numbers are passed to the messaging provider solely in order to deliver a message.

SMS consent and mobile information are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Removal is on request. See the privacy notice for what is held and for how long.
